Drop card and fighter images here (PNG, JPG, WEBP or SVG).
Then put the filename in the "art" column of the Cards, Styles or Opponents tab,
for example:  ps_jab.png
A full https:// link to an image also works.
Leave the column blank to keep the generated pastel art.
Cards show art at roughly 16:10; fighter portraits are cropped to a circle.

Cut-in art lives in art/cutins/, one folder per fighter. Each folder's
README.txt lists the exact file names to drop in and what each picture shows.
The paths are already in the CutIns tab, so there is nothing to edit: add the
files and upload. See art/cutins/README.txt for format and sizes.
